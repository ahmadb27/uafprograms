Degree levels

UAF runs the usual ladder, from a first degree through to a doctorate. Duration and credit load can differ by program, so treat these as the general picture.

BSc (Hons)

4 years · 8 semesters

The classic UAF degree, most famously in Agriculture. Students pick a specialization later in the program.

BS

4 years

Science, computing, business and social science programs such as Computer Science, Biotechnology and Economics.

DVM

5 years

Doctor of Veterinary Medicine, taught in the Faculty of Veterinary Science.

MSc (Hons)

2 years

Postgraduate study with a major field, coursework and a research thesis.

MPhil

Around 2 years

Research-heavy degree, often a step toward a PhD or a career in research and teaching.

PhD

3+ years

Doctoral research under a supervisor, ending in a dissertation and defense.

How admission generally works

The details (dates, fees, weightage) are published each year, so always confirm on the official UAF admissions page. The usual path looks like this.

Check eligibility

Each program asks for a particular intermediate or previous degree. Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering and ICS students qualify for different sets of programs.

Apply online

Applications open once a year on the university's admission portal. Keep your marks sheets, CNIC or B-form, and photographs ready.

Sit the entry test

Most undergraduate programs use an entry test alongside your previous marks to calculate an aggregate.

Watch the merit lists

Selected candidates are listed by program. Lists often come out in rounds, so a missed first list isn't always the end.

Confirm and enroll

Pay the fee and submit your documents within the deadline, or the seat is released to the next candidate.

Common questions

Is UAF only for agriculture students?

No. Agriculture is its core, but the university also teaches veterinary science, computing, business, food science, engineering and several pure sciences.

Can I change my major later?

In the BSc (Hons) Agriculture program, students choose a specialization after the first few semesters. Switching between faculties is a different matter and depends on university rules and seat availability.

Where do graduates end up?

Common routes include agricultural departments and extension work, research institutes, food and feed companies, banks with agri lending, teaching, private practice for veterinarians, and further study abroad.

Where do I find official fees and dates?

On the university's own website and admission notices. Fees and deadlines change each session, so don't rely on a third-party page, including this one.

Heads up: this is an unofficial guide. Program lists, durations and admission rules can change. Please confirm everything with the University of Agriculture, Faisalabad before you apply.
